# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Press **V** while dead and spectating to possess a random valuable on the
  level. You do not choose the item. It could be a mug next to the friend being
  chased, or a statue in an empty corridor.
- **WASD** rolls the item along the floor, **Space** charges a jump, **LMB**
  rattles to pull monsters, **E** leaves the item.
- Speed is the weapon. There is no attack key: a fast enough item damages
  monsters, flattens living teammates and shatters other loot on the way
  through.
- The possessed item never loses money, no matter how hard you throw it around.
  Deadly pits, lava and crushers are the exception - there it dies and your bar
  is wiped.
- Everything runs on one bar shared with the vanilla severed head, so a dead
  player has a single tank: haunt through an item, or push your head to the
  truck and get revived. The Death Head Battery upgrade makes both longer.
- While a living player carries the possessed item, they say something sad
  about it. Everyone in the lobby needs the mod for those lines to be heard.
- Spinning previews of real valuables from the level float above the spectate
  head, so a dead player can see the mod exists before the bar is full.
- Every number is in `BepInEx/config/sweet.iamitem.cfg`.
